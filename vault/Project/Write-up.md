# Write-up

One page, submitted with the code and video. Worth most of the 15 Communication points and it is where "Testing and honesty" is read. Owner: Product and story lead.
Start at feature freeze (Sun 14:00). Draft it ourselves first, then let others and AI tools edit.

Budget: about 450 words. If it doesn't fit on one page, cut words, not font size. No names, no school.

---

## Title
The project name and one line that says what it does.

## Problem (about 70 words)
Who has this problem, and why does it matter? One concrete situation.

## Approach (about 130 words)
The data we used, the model we chose and why, and how the pieces fit together. Mention what we tried that didn't work if it explains a choice.

## Results (about 130 words)
The main metric, our score **next to the baseline**, on the held-out test set. One sentence on what that means for the user. One small table or chart if it fits.

## Where it fails and what's next (about 80 words)
The kinds of mistakes it makes, what we didn't test, and the first thing we'd do with more time.

## Credits (about 40 words)
Datasets, pre-trained models, key libraries, and how we used AI tools. From Credits.
