# Hackathon Brief

Source: https://highschoolhackathon.org (read 13 Nov 2026). If the starter kit email says something different, the email wins. Update this note.

## What it is
A free, online, 48-hour AI hackathon for high school students, run by Veritas AI. Teams of 1 to 3. You build a working AI project: a model that predicts, classifies, generates or recommends something, applied to a real problem in the track you choose.

## What we submit
1. **Working code.** A Google Colab notebook or a GitHub repo the reviewers can open and run.
2. **A one-page write-up**: the problem, our approach, and how well it worked.
3. **A 2-minute demo video.** Unlisted YouTube link or direct upload.

## Ground rules
- All project code must be written during the 48 hours. Learning, practising and forming the team beforehand is fine.
- Public datasets, open-source libraries and pre-trained models are allowed. Credit what you use.
- AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude are allowed "the same way working engineers do": to debug, explain concepts or speed up routine code. We must understand and be able to explain everything in the project. Finalists are asked about their choices live.
- Late submissions are not accepted, with no exceptions for technical problems in the final minutes. The organisers recommend submitting at least an hour early.

## Judging
- **Round 1:** every submission is reviewed blind by the Veritas AI team against the rubric. Reviewers don't see names or schools.
- **Finals:** a 5-minute live demo plus questions from a panel of AI researchers and engineers.
- Final ranking = 70% finals score + 30% Round 1 score.
